## Boundaries

- Upstream names and links identify the subjects of analysis; they do not imply endorsement.
- Short quotations, if any, must be necessary, attributed, and clearly marked.
- Local `source/` directories are excluded from Git and must not be copied into a public release.
- License information is factual metadata about upstream repositories, not a relicensing of their work.
